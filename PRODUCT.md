# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

All MMMHMC employees use the product to access their own workforce records and employee self-service portal. HR and Accounting are the primary operational users and use the system more extensively for human resources and payroll work.

## Product Purpose

MMMHMC HRIS & Payroll provides one authenticated system for maintaining workforce records, completing HR operations, running payroll, and giving employees secure access to their own information.

## Operating Context

The product supports recurring workplace tasks including employee record management, attendance and daily time records, scheduling, leave, payroll processing, payslips, and employee self-service. The sign-in page is a frequent-use entry point rather than a public marketing destination.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the existing Laravel web authentication contract, including employee ID, password, remember-me behavior, validation, and authenticated redirects.
- HR and Accounting require efficient access to operational HRIS and payroll functions.
- Employees require a clear route into their personal records and self-service features.
- Workforce and payroll information is sensitive and access is restricted to authorized personnel.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: MMMHMC HRIS & Payroll.
- Preserve the existing MMMHMC logo assets under `public/assets/brand/`.
- Communication should feel clear, trustworthy, and appropriate for an institutional workforce system.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing brand logo and icon assets in `public/assets/brand/`.
- Existing application routes, views, and workflows provide the source of truth for supported capabilities.
- No testimonials, public performance claims, or promotional proof points have been supplied and none should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Make frequent employee tasks quick to find and start.
- Give HR and Accounting a dependable operational entry point.
- Protect sensitive workforce and payroll information.
- Use plain language and preserve established workplace terminology.
- Keep employee self-service understandable without obscuring the system's operational depth.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The sign-in experience must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion preferences, responsive layouts, and readable contrast for a broad employee population.
